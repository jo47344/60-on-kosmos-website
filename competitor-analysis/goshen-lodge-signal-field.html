      What they're doing right

      • One business shown as four properties. Goodwood, Barnard (Bellville), Spencer and Fitzroy each have their own Google listing and booking-site pages. That's four map pins and four review counts instead of one.
      • A separate site for each kind of guest. goshenlodge.com is the main site, sacontractors. is for contractors, and hourlyrooms. is for short stays. Each has its own blog and location pages, such as "Guest House in Goodwood" and "Our Locations".
      • A price per person, in simple tiers. Premium R350, Standard R300, Budget R250 per person per night, sharing. A site manager can multiply that by their crew size in their head.
      • A capacity number. "Group bookings of up to 102 guests" tells a big contractor straight away that they can house the whole crew.
      • Built for how contractors' companies pay. They advertise one monthly invoice for the whole stay, no or low deposit, utilities included, weekly linen, and bulk discounts from 10 beds.
      • Everyday comforts written as specifications. 100+ Mbps Wi-Fi, a 32" Smart TV, secure parking and a self-catering kitchen in every tier.
      • Listed nearly everywhere. Booking.com, Expedia, Hotels.com, Trip.com, LekkeSlaap and TravelGround, plus Facebook.

      Where they're weak

      • Low scores on the booking sites. Booking.com rates Fitzroy 5.6 ("Fair") from 69 reviews and Spencer 6.6 from 15. On LekkeSlaap, Spencer scores 2.9/5 and the host 2.8/5.
      • Their contractor site barely ranks. Of about 163 organic visits a month in South Africa, 97% go to the hourly-rooms site. The contractor site ranks for 1 keyword and gets about 5 visits.
      • Their details don't agree with each other. Different pages give contractor prices from R250 or R300, different phone numbers (078 240 8278 and 087 265 5005), and different lists of locations. Google trusts a business less when its details don't match across the web.
      • Their social media is quiet. The Facebook pages show 0 reviews, and their Instagram activity couldn't be confirmed.
      • Their brand is split. The hourly-rooms site shares a name with the contractor site, which can put corporate bookers off.

      Where Goshen's search traffic comes from

      Their best keywords are all hourly-room searches, e.g. "hourly rooms cape town" (#1), "hourly rooms in goodwood" (#1) and "rooms per hour near me" (#10, 1,000 searches a month). None is a contractor keyword. Semrush had no data for 60onkosmos.vercel.app, because sites on a shared vercel.app address are rarely tracked.

      What 60 on Kosmos should do next

      1. Lead with the price per person. Put "From R280 per person per night, sharing" above the fold and show tiers by room type, with a calculator: crew size × nights = monthly total.
      2. Show your capacity. State your total beds plainly, e.g. "Sleeps up to X contractors", and add a "whole-crew" quote form.
      3. Publish the paperwork. One monthly tax invoice, VAT, deposit terms, purchase orders accepted, utilities included. Corporate bookers look for these.
      4. Be listed wherever contractors look. List on LekkeSlaap, TravelGround and Booking.com with the same name, address and phone number everywhere. Your clean, ensuite rooms can beat Goshen's 5.6–6.6 scores.
      5. Get Google reviews on purpose. Goshen has 42–156 per property. Ask every crew foreman for a review at checkout, using a QR code on the room door.
      6. Move to your own domain. A vercel.app address limits how much Google trusts the site and isn't tracked by SEO tools. Point your 40 location pages at a .co.za.
      7. Take the contractor keywords they've left open. Their contractor site ranks for 1 keyword, so "contractor accommodation Bellville / Parow / Goodwood / Stikland" are there for the taking.
