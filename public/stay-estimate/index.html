A few useful answers

What if people arrive on different dates?

This estimate assumes the same adults and room arrangement for the whole stay. For staggered arrivals, departures, staff rotations, mixed private-room needs or a shutdown break, send the full schedule for a combined written quote. Separate estimates may allocate the same room twice.

Can a supervisor have a private room?

Yes. Choose “Supervisor privately + crew sharing”. We reserve Green Double for one adult and suggest suitable crew rooms. Include the supervisor in your total adult count. For more than one private room or a specific Twin/Triple preference, ask for a tailored quote.

Can we reserve all four guest rooms?

Yes, subject to availability. Choose “All four guest rooms for our group” to estimate the cost of reserving every guest room, including any unused rooms. There will be no unrelated overnight guests. Our caretaker lives on site and assists with arrival and property upkeep; shared facilities and house rules apply.

This option reserves all guest rooms. It does not promise exclusive use of the entire property or 24-hour assistance.

Comparing us with a house or room rental?

Check whether the quoted rental includes furniture, beds, bedding, utilities, Wi-Fi and cleaning. Depending on the property, you may need to arrange these separately, along with deposits and lease commitments.

At 60 on Kosmos, these essentials are already arranged. You book the dates and room setup you need, subject to our booking terms.

Why does my per-person price change?

Rooms are priced as complete rooms. The average depends on the beds used, length of stay and whether someone needs a private room. A Triple Room retains its room minimum even if only one or two adults use it. Four adults sharing both Twin rooms can qualify for a multi-room rate.

Is this live room availability?

No. The suggested arrangement uses our room layouts to estimate cost. We’ll check your dates and confirm the available rooms before issuing a written quote. If that arrangement is unavailable, your quote may differ.

What if our project gets extended?

Send the additional dates and we’ll confirm availability and pricing for the new booked period. A possible extension does not reserve future dates or change the price of the current booking.

Can one person or a couple stay?

Yes. Select one adult for a private room estimate. For two adults sharing a double bed, select two adults and tick the double-bed sharing option. We will confirm the preferred room in your written quote.

Where are you based?

60 Kosmos Street, Bellville South, Cape Town. Please check that our location suits your work site before booking.
